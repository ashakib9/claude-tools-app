Privacy policy

Claude Tools is used only by the owner of the Google account that authorizes it, on that owner's own computer.

  • Data read from Google (mail, files, calendar and contacts) is stored only on the owner's computer. To carry out the owner's own requests, parts of it are sent to the AI assistant the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) under the owner's own account.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's computer with restricted file permissions.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
